About Our Youth Ministry

Welcome to Mary Immaculate Kagaa Youths. We are a Catholic youth community committed to growing spiritually, supporting one another, serving the Church, and sharing God's love.

Through prayer, fellowship, learning, and service, we seek to become responsible young Christians who make a positive difference in society.

Our Youth Activities

Prayer and Faith

Growing closer to God through prayer, Mass, Scripture, and spiritual formation.

Fellowship

Building friendships, encouraging each other, and sharing our experiences in faith.

Community Service

Showing Christ's love through charity, volunteering, and helping those in need.

Youth Events

Taking part in youth meetings, retreats, conferences, and other church activities.
